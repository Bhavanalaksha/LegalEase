### Deep-Dive Empathy Analysis

#### 1. What the User Says
- *"I just need a simple, standard NDA for my new client, why does it have to be an ordeal?"*
- *"I cannot afford a $400 legal consultation for every single freelance project."*
- *"Online PDF templates always look messy and are hard to edit."*

#### 2. What the User Thinks & Feels
- **Fear:** Accidental omission of critical clauses (like governing law, IP transfer, or confidentiality).
- **Frustration:** Spending non-billable hours wrestling with formatting, headers, and legal phrasing.
- **Aspiration:** Presenting a polished, branded, corporate image to clients and tenants.

#### 3. What the User Does
- Searches free legal template repositories, only to encounter paywalls or outdated 2012 documents.
- Manually copies and pastes clauses across various documents, risking inconsistent definitions and formatting errors.
- Downloads files in formats they cannot easily convert or print cleanly.

#### 4. Pains vs. Gains

| Pains (Frictions & Fears) | Gains (Aspirations & Desired Relief) |
| :--- | :--- |
| High attorney hourly billing rates | Instant, free/affordable document generation on-demand |
| Lengthy delays waiting for turnaround | Near-zero latency with AI (under 10 seconds) |
| Risk of invalid or missing standard clauses | Guaranteed inclusion of recitals, severability, and jurisdiction |
| Disjointed file conversion (.txt to Word or PDF) | Instant 1-click download in Plain Text, Word (.docx), or Branded PDF |
