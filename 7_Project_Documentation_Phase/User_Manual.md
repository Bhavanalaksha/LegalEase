# Phase 7: Project Documentation Phase
## Document: End-User Manual & Step-by-Step Guide

---

### Welcome to LegalEase!
**LegalEase** is your AI-powered legal drafting assistant. This guide walks you through drafting, customizing, and downloading professional contracts in minutes.

---

### Step 1: Launch the Application
1. Open your web browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:8501
   ```
2. You will see the **LegalEase: AI Legal Document Generator** interface with a centered scales-of-justice logo and intuitive input form.

---

### Step 2: Choose a Scenario or Input Details

You have two simple ways to generate a contract:

#### Option A: Quick-Fill Scenario Preset
1. Click the **"⚡ Quick Fill with Sample Scenarios"** expander.
2. Select your desired archetype:
   - *Freelance Work Contract*
   - *Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)*
   - *Residential Lease Agreement*
   - *Employment Offer Letter*
3. Click **"Apply Template Values"** to pre-populate the form.

#### Option B: Custom Input
Directly enter your details into the form:
- **Document Type:** E.g., `Freelance Work Contract`, `Consulting Agreement`.
- **Parties Involved:** E.g., `Jane Doe (Service Provider), TechNova Inc. (Client)`.
- **Terms & Conditions:** Use semicolons to separate distinct rules or clauses:
  ```
  Work must be delivered by May 15, 2025;
  Payment will be made within 7 days of invoice;
  The client retains intellectual property rights;
  Confidentiality must be maintained at all times;
  ```
- **Effective Date:** E.g., `April 15, 2025`.

---

### Step 3: Generate the Document
1. Click the primary **"🚀 Generate Document"** button.
2. The system contacts the Gemini AI core (or standard legal fallback engine).
3. A green notification **"✅ Document Generated Successfully!"** appears.
4. An official **dark-themed preview card** displays the full contract with headings, numbered recitals, covenants, and signature blocks.

---

### Step 4: Review and Edit (Optional)
1. If you wish to customize wording, click **"📝 Click to Edit Document"**.
2. A large text editor expands with the complete contract text.
3. Add any custom clauses or modify dates. Your edits automatically synchronize with all download formats!

---

### Step 5: Download in Your Preferred Format
Choose from three formats:
1. **📄 Download as .TXT:** Instant plain text file for emails or notes.
2. **📘 Download as .DOCX:** Formatted Word document with top logo, Times New Roman font, summary terms table, and legal footer.
3. **📕 Download as .PDF:** Ready-to-print branded PDF with clean headers and footers on every page.
